      How it works

      Three steps with zero text uploaded to any server: masking and restoration happen in volatile tab RAM.

      1. Local masking on your device

      Deterministic detectors find contacts, bank numbers, IDs, and secrets directly in the page and swap them for short tags.

      2. AI only sees placeholder tags

      Only scrubbed text goes to the AI chat, while the replacement table stays strictly inside volatile tab memory.

      3. Instant restoration in the reply

      Paste the AI reply into Step 2 — PromptVeil swaps original names, accounts, and numbers back in a single pass.

      What we don't catch yet

      Honest boundaries of client-side rules — so you know what to review manually or add to Custom Words.

      Unlisted prose names & project codenames

      In Normal mode, rare surnames, nicknames, and internal project codenames are not auto-masked to avoid false positives.

      Scans, photos & image-only PDFs (OCR)

      The Free tier handles plain-text files up to 2 MB; Pro adds .docx, .xlsx, and text-layer .pdf files up to 50 MB, while image OCR is not performed locally.

      Typos, transliteration & split digits

      Card digits broken up with letters or extra spaces and unconventional address abbreviations may pass through unmasked.

      Custom internal IDs & domain secrets

      Short passwords, ticket numbers, SKUs, and internal identifiers without a fixed pattern should be added as custom rules.

      Add your own terms or apply a starter industry pack — custom rules always mask with high priority.
